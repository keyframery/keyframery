With `<Cuts />` in your root layout, Keyframery finds shadcn's own parts on the page and animates them. You don't import anything, and nothing in `components/ui` changes.

## Dialogs, alert dialogs and the command menu

A dialog grows out of the button that opened it, and closes back into it. Alert dialogs and the command menu do the same.

## Menus, popovers and the rest

Popover, dropdown menu, context menu, menubar, select, combobox, hover card, tooltip, navigation menu, accordion and collapsible already open from their trigger. Keyframery gives them the same speed and easing as everything else.

## Buttons, checkboxes, switches and sliders

Controls you press, tick or drag answer on the control itself. These are responses, not cuts:

- **Press:** buttons, toggles, pagination links and carousel arrows dip while pressed, quicker in than out.
- **Toggle:** a checkbox's tick draws itself, a radio dot grows from its centre, a switch thumb travels on your curve and stretches while held, and choice cards change colour instead of jumping.
- **Drag:** a slider thumb grows while you drag it, and a resize handle thickens.
- **Fill:** a progress bar eases to its new value.
- **Highlight:** the selection in a toggle group, pagination, the sidebar and the calendar glides to the item you click. The sidebar's width follows your curve, and its labels leave before it closes.
- **Error:** a field that turns invalid after a submit gives one short shake, and its error message rises in. Never while you type.
- **Arrivals:** an alert, a combobox chip or an attachment that appears on its own eases in, and an avatar's photo dissolves in over its initials. A finished upload gives a small punch and a failed one shakes.
- **Steps and months:** a calendar's next month and a questionnaire's next question slide in from the side you went toward, like tabs.
- **Icons:** ticks in menus, the command palette and comboboxes draw themselves, the accordion's chevron turns instead of being swapped, a close ✕ turns while pressed, arrows nudge toward where they go, and a toast's or alert dialog's icon plays once as it arrives. For each icon's own move when it's pressed (a gear turns, a bell rings), add [Icon moves](/docs/helpers/icon-moves).

Controls that load already checked don't redraw, and anything moved from the keyboard (arrow keys in a calendar, a slider's steps) stays instant, because people repeat those many times in a row. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns every response off and keeps the cuts.

## What stays still

Labels, separators, keyboard shortcuts and other parts that never change stay as they are. Motion there would only be noise.

To animate lists, numbers, skeleton loading, whole-content states or a card that opens into a page, use the five helpers under **Animate your app** in the sidebar. The [theme builder](/theme) lets you preview the automatic components and helpers together.
