## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| A button is pressed | Dips to 97% | 70 ms in, 150 ms out | Automatic |

## Install

Button Group is shadcn's own component. With `<Cuts />` in your root layout ([Quick start](/docs/installation)), there's nothing else to add:

```bash
npx shadcn add button-group
```

## Turn it off

Put `data-cut="none"` on a button group (or anything around it) to keep shadcn's own motion there. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns off every response across the app and keeps the cuts. With reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.
