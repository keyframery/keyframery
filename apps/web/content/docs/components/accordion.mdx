## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| A section opens or closes | The height eases open and closed | 200 ms | Automatic |
| The section's chevron | Turns a half turn with the section | 200 ms | Automatic |

## Install

Accordion is shadcn's own component. With `<Cuts />` in your root layout ([Quick start](/docs/installation)), there's nothing else to add:

```bash
npx shadcn add accordion
```

## Turn it off

Put `data-cut="none"` on a accordion (or anything around it) to keep shadcn's own motion there. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns off every response across the app and keeps the cuts. With reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.
