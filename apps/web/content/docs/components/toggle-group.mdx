## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| An item is pressed | Dips to 97% | 70 ms in, 150 ms out | Automatic |
| A single choice moves | The selection glides to it | 200–320 ms | Automatic |

A selection moved with the arrow keys stays instant.

## Install

Toggle Group is shadcn's own component. With `<Cuts />` in your root layout ([Quick start](/docs/installation)), there's nothing else to add:

```bash
npx shadcn add toggle-group
```

## Turn it off

Put `data-cut="none"` on a toggle group (or anything around it) to keep shadcn's own motion there. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns off every response across the app and keeps the cuts. With reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.
