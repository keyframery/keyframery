## Which cut it uses

Toasts use the **Cut on action** cut. A cut is how something arrives on the screen and how it leaves. The toast starts at the button you pressed, small and a little blurred, and flies to its corner, so you can see which button it is about without reading it.

<CutCards menu="toast">

| Cut | What you see | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Cut on action (default) | Flies out of the button you pressed, then settles into its corner. | `<Cuts toast="cut-on-action" />` |

</CutCards>

## What it fixes

A stock toast slides in from the corner, far from the button that caused it. With cut on action, it starts at the button, small and slightly blurred, and flies to its place. You see which action the toast is about without reading it.

If nothing was pressed in the last second, as with a toast from a websocket message, the library's own entrance plays.

It works with sonner on both bases, and with Base UI's own `Toast`.

## Install

```npm
npx shadcn add sonner
```

## Change it

Every change is one prop. The controls at the top of the page try them live.

### Make it faster or slower

`pace` multiplies every duration: `1.6` is slower, `0.6` is faster.

```tsx
// In your root layout: every motion in your app
<Cuts pace={1.6} />
```

The flight takes 480 ms × pace, from 0.4 scale and a 6px blur. The toast's exit is the library's own.

### Turn it off

```tsx
// In your root layout: toasts keep the library's own entrance
<Cuts toast="none" />
```

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.

## Props

**On `<Cuts />`**, in your root layout:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `toast` | `"cut-on-action" \| "none"` | `"cut-on-action"` | The cut for sonner and Base UI toasts. |
| `pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration in your app. |

**In your global CSS:**

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kf-ease` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The curve things arrive on. Or pick a theme at [/theme](/theme). |
| `--kf-ease-exit` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` | The curve things leave on. |
