## Which cut it uses

Sheets use the **Slide and sink** cut. A cut is how something arrives on the screen and how it leaves. The sheet slides in from its side while the page behind it steps back a little, so you can tell the sheet is on top. There are 3 to pick from, and each one is a single prop:

<CutCards menu="sheet">

| Cut | What you see | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Slide and sink (default) | Slides in from its side while the page behind it steps back a little. | `<Cuts sheet="slide-sink" />` |
| Slide | Slides in from its side. The page behind stays still. | `<Cuts sheet="slide" />` |
| Fade | Fades in where it stands. Nothing moves. | `<Cuts sheet="fade" />` |

</CutCards>

## What it fixes

A stock sheet slides over a page that doesn't react, so the two look flat against each other. With `slide-sink`, the page behind scales to 0.975, dims slightly and rounds its corners while the sheet arrives. Now the sheet reads as on top. When it closes, the page comes forward again.

The part of the page that steps back is the top-level element in `<body>` that holds the button you pressed. A sheet opened from inside another sheet doesn't sink the page twice.

## Install

```npm
npx shadcn add sheet
```

## Change it

Every change is one prop. The controls at the top of the page try them live.

### Change just one

For just one sheet, put `data-cut` on its `SheetContent`:

```tsx
<SheetContent data-cut="slide" />
```

### Make it faster or slower

`pace` multiplies every duration: `1.6` is slower, `0.6` is faster.

```tsx
// In your root layout: every motion in your app
<Cuts pace={1.6} />

// Or just this one
<SheetContent data-cut-pace="1.6" />
```

### Turn it off

```tsx
// In your root layout: no motion on any sheet
<Cuts sheet="none" />

// Just this one keeps shadcn's own motion
<SheetContent data-cut="none" />
```

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.

## Props

**On `<Cuts />`**, in your root layout:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sheet` | `"slide-sink" \| "slide" \| "fade" \| "none"` | `"slide-sink"` | The cut for every sheet. |
| `pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration in your app. |

**On one `SheetContent`**, for just that one:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data-cut` | `"slide-sink" \| "slide" \| "fade" \| "none"` | – | The cut for this sheet. Beats the `<Cuts>` prop. |
| `data-cut-pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration of this one. |

**In your global CSS:**

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kf-ease` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The curve things arrive on. Or pick a theme at [/theme](/theme). |
| `--kf-ease-exit` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` | The curve things leave on. |

## Timing

| Cut | Opening | Closing |
|---|---|---|
| `slide-sink` | from its edge over 460 ms, and the page steps back | 260 ms, and the page returns |
| `slide` | the same, without the page stepping back | same |
| `fade` | opacity only, 200 ms | 150 ms |
