## Which cut it uses

Tabs use the **J-cut**. A cut is how something arrives on the screen and how it leaves. With a J-cut, the highlight slides to the tab you picked first, and the new panel follows a beat later, so your eye is already in the right place when it arrives. There are 3 to pick from, and each one is a single prop:

<CutCards menu="tabs">

| Cut | What you see | Code |
|---|---|---|
| J-cut (default) | The highlight slides to your tab first, then the new panel follows a beat later. | `<Cuts tabs="j-cut" />` |
| Whip | A quick swipe with a little motion blur, all at once. | `<Cuts tabs="whip" />` |
| Fade | The old panel fades out and the new one fades in. | `<Cuts tabs="fade" />` |

</CutCards>

## What it fixes

Stock tabs swap their panel in a single frame, and the frame snaps to the new height. With the J-cut, the active pill whips across first, the old panel leaves, and the new panel enters 70 ms later. Meanwhile the frame's height morphs smoothly. Your eye follows the pill, and the panel arrives where you're already looking.

## Install

```npm
npx shadcn add tabs
```

## Change it

Every change is one prop. The controls at the top of the page try them live.

### Change just one

For just one tabs component, put `data-cut` on its `Tabs` root:

```tsx
<Tabs data-cut="fade" defaultValue="account">…</Tabs>
```

### Make it faster or slower

`pace` multiplies every duration: `1.6` is slower, `0.6` is faster.

```tsx
// In your root layout: every motion in your app
<Cuts pace={1.6} />

// Or just this one
<Tabs data-cut-pace="1.6">…</Tabs>
```

### Turn it off

```tsx
// In your root layout: no motion on any tabs
<Cuts tabs="none" />

// Just this one keeps shadcn's own motion
<Tabs data-cut="none">…</Tabs>
```

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.

## Props

**On `<Cuts />`**, in your root layout:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tabs` | `"j-cut" \| "whip" \| "fade" \| "none"` | `"j-cut"` | The cut for every tabs component. |
| `pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration in your app. |

**On one `Tabs`**, for just that one:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data-cut` | `"j-cut" \| "whip" \| "fade" \| "none"` | – | The cut for this tabs component. Beats the `<Cuts>` prop. |
| `data-cut-pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration of this one. |

**In your global CSS:**

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kf-ease` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The curve things arrive on. Or pick a theme at [/theme](/theme). |
| `--kf-ease-exit` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` | The curve things leave on. |

## Timing

| Cut | What plays |
|---|---|
| `j-cut` | the pill whips over (200 ms), the old panel leaves (180 ms), the new panel enters 70 ms later (300 ms), and the height morphs (320 ms) |
| `whip` | no lead, 160 ms, with a 4px motion blur on both panels and the height morph |
| `fade` | a 180 ms crossfade and the height morph |
