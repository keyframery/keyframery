## Which cut it uses

Input Group doesn't open or close, so it has no cut. It gets a **response** instead: a small reaction when you press it, tick it or drag it, so you can feel that it worked.

## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Its input turns invalid after a submit | The group shakes once | 240 ms | Automatic |
| Its buttons | Dip while pressed | 70 ms in, 150 ms out | Automatic |

## Install

Input Group is shadcn's own component. With `<Cuts />` in your root layout ([Quick start](/docs/installation)), there's nothing else to add:

```npm
npx shadcn add input-group
```

## Change it

Every change is one prop. The controls at the top of the page try them live.

### Make it faster or slower

`pace` multiplies every duration: `1.6` is slower, `0.6` is faster.

```tsx
// In your root layout: every motion in your app
<Cuts pace={1.6} />

// Or just this one
<InputGroup data-cut-pace="1.6" />
```

### Turn it off

```tsx
// In your root layout: no responses anywhere (cuts keep playing)
<Cuts responses="none" />

// Just this one keeps shadcn's own motion
<InputGroup data-cut="none" />
```

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.

## Props

**On `<Cuts />`**, in your root layout:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration in your app. |
| `responses` | `"on" \| "none"` | `"on"` | "none" turns off every response and keeps the cuts. |

**On one `InputGroup`**, for just that one:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data-cut` | `"none"` | – | `none` keeps shadcn's own motion on this one. |
| `data-cut-pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration of this one. |

**In your global CSS:**

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kf-ease` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The curve things arrive on. Or pick a theme at [/theme](/theme). |
| `--kf-ease-exit` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` | The curve things leave on. |
