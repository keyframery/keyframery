## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pressed | Dips to 97% | 70 ms in, 150 ms out | Automatic |
| Its icon, when pressed | Plays its own move | 300–700 ms | [Icon moves](/docs/helpers/icon-moves) |

## Install

Toggle is shadcn's own component. With `<Cuts />` in your root layout ([Quick start](/docs/installation)), there's nothing else to add:

```bash
npx shadcn add toggle
```

For each icon's own move when it's pressed, add [Icon moves](/docs/helpers/icon-moves): `npx shadcn add @keyframery/icon-moves`.

## Turn it off

Put `data-cut="none"` on a toggle (or anything around it) to keep shadcn's own motion there. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns off every response across the app and keeps the cuts. With reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.
