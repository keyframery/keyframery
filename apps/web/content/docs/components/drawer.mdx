## Which cut it uses

Drawers use the **Slide and sink** cut. A cut is how something arrives on the screen and how it leaves. The drawer keeps its own swipe and spring. Keyframery only makes the page behind step back a little while the drawer rises, so the drawer reads as on top.

<CutCards menu="drawer">

| Cut | What you see | Code |
|---|---|---|
| Slide and sink (default) | The page behind steps back a little while the drawer slides up. | `<Cuts drawer="slide-sink" />` |

</CutCards>

## What it fixes

Drawers already have good motion: they follow your finger. What they're missing is depth. With `slide-sink`, the page behind steps back as the drawer rises, the way iOS sheets do. The drawer's own swipe and spring are never touched.

It works with both drawers: Base UI's and the Radix base's Vaul drawer.

## Install

```npm
npx shadcn add drawer
```

## Change it

Every change is one prop. The controls at the top of the page try them live.

### Make it faster or slower

`pace` multiplies every duration: `1.6` is slower, `0.6` is faster.

```tsx
// In your root layout: every motion in your app
<Cuts pace={1.6} />

// Or just this one
<DrawerContent data-cut-pace="1.6" />
```

### Turn it off

```tsx
// In your root layout: the page stays still behind every drawer
<Cuts drawer="none" />

// Just this one
<DrawerContent data-cut="none" />
```

With reduced motion turned on in the system settings, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.

## Props

**On `<Cuts />`**, in your root layout:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `drawer` | `"slide-sink" \| "none"` | `"slide-sink"` | Whether the page steps back behind drawers. |
| `pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration in your app. |

**On one `DrawerContent`**, for just that one:

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data-cut` | `"slide-sink" \| "none"` | – | `none` keeps the page still for this drawer. |
| `data-cut-pace` | `number` | `1` | Multiplies every duration of this one. |

**In your global CSS:**

| Prop | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--kf-ease` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | The curve things arrive on. Or pick a theme at [/theme](/theme). |
| `--kf-ease-exit` | `string` | `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0.75, 0)` | The curve things leave on. |
