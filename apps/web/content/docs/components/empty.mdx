## What moves

| When | What you see | Timing | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Empty becomes content, or back | Fade or slide, with the height easing | 260 ms | [StateCut](/docs/helpers/state-cut) |

## Install

Install [StateCut](/docs/helpers/state-cut) and wrap the part that changes: `npx shadcn add @keyframery/state-cut`.

## Turn it off

Put `data-cut="none"` on a empty (or anything around it) to keep shadcn's own motion there. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns off every response across the app and keeps the cuts. With reduced motion, movement becomes a short fade or nothing.
