## Unreleased

- Responses: buttons dip while pressed, ticks draw themselves, radio dots grow, switch thumbs stretch, slider thumbs and resize handles grow while dragged, and progress bars ease to their value. `<Cuts responses="none" />` turns them off
- Highlight: the selection glides in toggle groups, pagination, the sidebar and the calendar (instant from the keyboard); the sidebar's width follows the theme curve and its labels leave first
- Errors: a field that turns invalid after a submit shakes once and its message rises in, never while typing
- Icons: ticks in menus, command and combobox draw themselves; the accordion chevron turns; a close ✕ turns and arrows nudge while pressed; toast and alert dialog icons play as they arrive
- Icon moves (`npx shadcn add @keyframery/icon-moves`): about 50 lucide icons play their own move when pressed; other outline icons redraw, filled ones punch

- Quiet, Crisp and Expressive motion profiles with a complete workflow preview
- Local theme saves, validated version-1 JSON import/export, share links and full installation and agent handoff
- Shared entrance and exit easing across the engine and helpers
- StateCut for whole-content state changes, with fade, slide, immediate swaps and reduced-motion support
- Documentation and agent guidance for reusable motion themes and five helpers

## 0.1.0 (8 October 2026)

The first release.

- `<Cuts />`: rack focus, punch-in and fade for dialog, alert dialog and command; slide-sink, slide and fade for sheet; slide-sink for drawer; J-cut, whip and fade for tabs; cut on action for sonner and Base UI toasts
- Eleven tuned components: popover, dropdown-menu, context-menu, menubar, select, combobox, hover-card, tooltip, navigation-menu, accordion and collapsible
- Helpers: MatchCut, ListCut, ValueCut and LoadCut
- Seven `--kf-*` variables, per-element `data-cut`, `data-cut-pace` and `data-cut-origin`, and the `keyframery:cut` event
- Reduced motion: every cut becomes a short fade
- Tested on Base UI and Radix, in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox. See [Compatibility](/docs/compatibility) for versions.
