Keyframery doesn't invent a new animation for each component. It has nine moves, and each component uses the few that fit. That is why fifty-five components feel like one product.

| Move | What it does | Where you see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stretch** | A marker travels by stretching: the leading edge leaves first, the trailing edge follows a beat later. | [Tabs](/docs/components/tabs), [Segmented Control](/docs/components/segmented-control), [Switch](/docs/components/switch), [Navigation Menu](/docs/components/navigation-menu) |
| **Glide** | One highlight moves between items instead of each item lighting up on its own. | [Menu](/docs/components/menu), [Select](/docs/components/select), [Radio](/docs/components/radio), [Calendar](/docs/components/calendar), [OTP Input](/docs/components/otp-input) |
| **Unfold** | A popup grows out of the control that opened it, from a soft blur into focus. | [Popover](/docs/components/popover), [Menu](/docs/components/menu), [Tooltip](/docs/components/tooltip), [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog) |
| **Cascade** | Rows arrive one after another, a few milliseconds apart. | [Menu](/docs/components/menu), [Dialog](/docs/components/dialog), [Timeline](/docs/components/timeline), [Accordion](/docs/components/accordion) |
| **Roll** | Digits roll to their new value like a counter. | [Meter](/docs/components/meter), [Progress](/docs/components/progress), [Slider](/docs/components/slider), [Number Input](/docs/components/number-input) |
| **Draw** | Checks, dashes and lines draw themselves stroke by stroke. | [Checkbox](/docs/components/checkbox), [Timeline](/docs/components/timeline), [Separator](/docs/components/separator) |
| **Press** | Anything you press sinks a little and springs back on release. | [Button](/docs/components/button), [Toggle](/docs/components/toggle), [Slider](/docs/components/slider) |
| **Direction** | New content comes in from the side you moved toward. | [Tabs](/docs/components/tabs), [Calendar](/docs/components/calendar), [Carousel](/docs/components/carousel), [Command](/docs/components/command) |
| **Height** | Content opens to its real height, never a guessed one. | [Accordion](/docs/components/accordion), [Collapsible](/docs/components/collapsible), [Tree](/docs/components/tree), [Field](/docs/components/field) |

## Why motion at all

Each move answers a question the interface would otherwise leave open:

- **Where did this come from?** A popup unfolds from the button that opened it.
- **Where did I go?** A panel slides in from the side you moved toward.
- **What changed?** A number rolls, so the change is seen and not missed.
- **Did that register?** A press sinks and springs back.

## Sleek, never childish

The moves are tuned to be noticed and then forgotten: no wobble, no confetti, nothing that pulses while idle. If the default has more spring than your product wants, [pick Calm or Snappy](/docs/motion-styles).
